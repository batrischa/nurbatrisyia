My Education Path

Click the photo!

I began my educational journey at Sekolah Kebangsaan Bandar Tun Hussein 2, which is located at Jalan Suadamai, Tun Hussein Onn, Cheras, Selangor. The school is situated right in front of a residential area, so most of the students came from there as well. I don’t have many memories from my primary school days because, I was pretty noob back then. Even so, I still managed to receive the Caring Student Award and was appointed as a Peer Counselor (PRS), despite the fact that I wasn't actually mentoring anyone toward good behavior.

Photo is clickable!

After completing my primary education, I continued my studies at Sekolah Menengah Kebangsaan Bandar Tun Hussein (2), which is located right next to SKBTHO2. As a result, most of the students here also came from the same primary school. This secondary school was slightly bigger than my primary school and had a very different atmosphere with more students and more activities. Compared to my primary school days, I could remember more moments in secondary school because by then I

already had a phone to take photos and record videos. Every memory in this school had its own value; some were funny and still make me laugh when I think about them, while others were a bit challenging. Form 5 was the toughest but also the most memorable time. Even though everyone had their own friend groups or small arguments sometimes, friendships were still formed and we spent a lot of time together. All these experiences became memories that I will never forget.

In order to pursue my passion, I continued my education at Universiti Teknologi MARA in Segamat, Johor, after completing my secondary education. University life was quite different from secondary school in that it provided more independence, greater personal responsibility, and a more scholarly and professional environment. I also had the opportunity to meet new friends from diverse backgrounds, participate in challenging projects and activities, and further develop my skills. My experiences at university not only strengthened my academic knowledge but also shaped

my maturity, confidence, and the way I approach challenges in real life. Every moment, from lectures to projects to extracurricular activities, provided me with significant memories and lessons that I will always appreciate.
